# Mandalaye Park — Website Redesign Concept

Ultra-premium, editorial-style website redesign concept for Mandalaye Park Estate, a family-run farm tourism and rural estate business on French Island, Victoria, Australia.

## About the business

- 142-acre rural estate on French Island, in Westernport Bay, about 1 hour 30 minutes from Melbourne.
- Family-run farm tourism, accommodation, weddings and special events. Established 2012.
- Contact: Celia Fisher — 0417 392 100 — c.fisher1@live.com — 39 Mandalaye Park Road, French Island VIC 3921.

## What's on the page

- Immersive hero, brand story, three experience cards (Farm & Island Experience, Country Accommodation, Weddings & Special Events)
- The Estate, four accommodation suites (Luxury Cottage, The Bell Tower Suite, The Manager's Quarters, The Field View) with links to their Airbnb listings
- Weddings & Special Events, French Island story (koalas, wetlands, historic chicory kiln c.1926, ferry travel), masonry photo gallery with lightbox, enquiry section

## Notes

- All content and photographs are taken from the existing Mandalaye Park website (mandalayepark.com). No prices, availability, testimonials, or business details have been invented.
- The enquiry form is a demo: it prepares a copyable summary and states plainly that nothing has been sent.
- Photos are hotlinked from the original website. The Muse artifact version of this concept uses enhanced, upscaled copies of the same photographs.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the repo to GitHub Pages / Vercel — it's a single self-contained static page.
