Our Story

Where Country Life Meets Island Escape

Mandalaye Park is a family-run estate set on 142 acres of rural French Island, in the middle of Westernport Bay — a place where the pace slows, the air clears, and country life is lived as it always has been.

Our suites sit in the grounds of the historic homestead, beneath magnificent oak trees where koalas rest in the treetops and peacocks wander at will. There are no through roads to anywhere else, no mains power or water — just solar, generators, open paddocks and island quiet, about an hour and a half from Melbourne yet a world away from it.

Come for a farm stay, a wedding beneath the oaks, or simply a few days of doing very little, beautifully.

French Island

An Island Like No Other

French Island sits in Westernport Bay, 61 km southeast of Melbourne — 170 square kilometres of wetlands, salt marshes, coastal heaths and stringybark forest, home to just 100 residents. Around 70% of the island is crown land, state and national parks, protecting over 480 species of indigenous plants.

Koalas — introduced in the 1880s and free of the diseases that affect mainland populations — flourish here in large numbers, alongside echidnas and our own peacocks. There are no foxes, no mains water or electricity; island life runs on solar, generators and goodwill.

On the estate stands our historic chicory kiln, built in the early 1900s when chicory was the island's main income for over 70 years. Some artefacts remain in place — and today a family of barn owls nests in its roof.

Getting to French Island

Western Port Ferries run daily from Stony Point pier (≈15 min) and Cowes, Phillip Island (≈20 min) to Tankerton Jetty. The passenger ferry doesn't carry vehicles — free parking is at Stony Point Foreshore, and Metro trains to Stony Point connect with sailings. The Spirit of French Island barge carries vehicles from Corinella. The island has 40+ km of gravel roads and tracks — bring a bike on the ferry, or simply walk.
